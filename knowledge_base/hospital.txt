CityCare General Hospital

CityCare General Hospital is located in Manchester, United Kingdom. It was founded
in 1985. The hospital has a capacity of 420 beds.

The Medical Director is Dr. Robert Evans. The Emergency Department Head is
Dr. Olivia Brown.

Departments include Emergency Medicine, Cardiology, Neurology, Pediatrics, and
Radiology.

The Radiology department operates 3 Siemens MRI scanners. The MRI department is
open Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Laboratory turnaround times: COVID PCR results are usually available within
24 hours. Blood test results are usually available within 6 hours. CT scan results
are usually available within 12 hours.

Emergency policy: patients with life-threatening conditions must be treated
immediately regardless of insurance status.

Visiting hours are 2 PM to 7 PM on weekdays and 10 AM to 7 PM on weekends.

Parking is free for emergency patients. General visitors pay 5 pounds per day.

The hospital pharmacy is open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM, and is closed
on Sundays.
