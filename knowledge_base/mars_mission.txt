Ares Horizon Mars Mission

The Ares Horizon mission is an uncrewed Mars exploration mission led by the
Interplanetary Research Consortium. The spacecraft launched on 14 March 2031 from
Cape Canaveral.

The mission's primary objective is to search for evidence of past microbial life in
the Jezero Crater region.

The rover, named Odyssey Rover, weighs approximately 1,050 kilograms and is powered
by a radioisotope thermoelectric generator, giving it an operational lifespan of at
least 14 years.

The journey from Earth to Mars took approximately 7 months, with the rover landing
on 22 October 2031.

Odyssey Rover carries seven scientific instruments, including a ground-penetrating
radar and a mass spectrometer.

The mission's total budget was approximately 2.7 billion dollars.

Mission Director Dr. Elena Vasquez leads the science team of over 200 researchers
from 15 countries.

The rover can travel at a maximum speed of 0.12 kilometers per hour and communicates
with Earth via the Mars Relay Satellite Network, with a signal delay of between
4 and 24 minutes depending on planetary alignment.
