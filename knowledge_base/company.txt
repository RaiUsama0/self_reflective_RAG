Nordvale Technologies

Nordvale Technologies is a software company headquartered in Stockholm, Sweden. It
was founded in 2011 by Erik Lindqvist and Anna Bergstrom.

The company specializes in cloud-based logistics software and currently employs
approximately 1,400 people across offices in Stockholm, Berlin, and Austin.

Nordvale went public on the Stockholm Stock Exchange in 2019. Its most recent annual
revenue was 310 million dollars.

The current Chief Executive Officer is Anna Bergstrom, who took over the role in
2020. Erik Lindqvist now serves as Chief Technology Officer.

Nordvale's flagship product, RouteFlow, is used by over 3,000 logistics companies
worldwide to optimize delivery routes.

The company was named one of the top 50 European technology employers in a recent
industry survey.

Nordvale operates a hybrid work policy, requiring employees to be in the office at
least 2 days per week.

In 2023, Nordvale acquired a smaller competitor, PathSync, for approximately
45 million dollars.
