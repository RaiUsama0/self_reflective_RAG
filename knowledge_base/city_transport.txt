Riverton Metropolitan Transit

Riverton Metropolitan Transit is the public transport authority for the city of
Riverton. It operates a network of 6 metro lines, 84 bus routes, and 3 tram lines.

The metro system opened in 1978 and now carries approximately 1.1 million
passengers per day.

A single metro fare costs 2.80 dollars. A monthly unlimited travel pass costs
95 dollars. Children under 5 travel free.

The transit authority introduced a fleet of 120 fully electric buses in 2022,
replacing older diesel vehicles as part of a plan to make the entire bus fleet
electric by 2030.

The longest metro line, the Blue Line, runs for 42 kilometers and has 38 stations.

Riverton Metropolitan Transit is overseen by Director Priya Chandrasekhar.

The system operates from 5:00 AM to 1:00 AM on weekdays, and 24 hours a day on
weekends.

Average metro train punctuality over the last reporting year was 94 percent.

The transit authority's annual operating budget is approximately 680 million
dollars, funded by a combination of fares and municipal subsidy.
