SkyBridge Airlines

SkyBridge Airlines is headquartered in Dublin, Ireland. The airline was founded in
2003 and began commercial operations in 2005.

SkyBridge operates a fleet of 62 aircraft, consisting of Airbus A320 and Airbus A321
models. The airline flies to 48 destinations across Europe and North Africa.

The Chief Executive Officer is Fiona Walsh.

Checked baggage allowance is 23 kilograms for Economy class and 32 kilograms for
Business class. Carry-on baggage is limited to 10 kilograms.

Standard check-in closes 45 minutes before departure for domestic flights and
60 minutes before departure for international flights.

SkyBridge's loyalty programme, SkyBridge Rewards, has three tiers: Silver, Gold,
and Platinum. Platinum members receive complimentary lounge access.

The airline's on-time performance for the most recent reporting year was 82 percent.

SkyBridge does not operate any long-haul routes; all flights are short-haul or
medium-haul within Europe and North Africa.

In-flight Wi-Fi is available on all Airbus A321 aircraft but not on the A320 fleet.
