Elmwood State University

Elmwood State University is located in Bristol, United Kingdom. It was founded in
1932. The university currently enrolls approximately 18,000 students.

The Chancellor is Professor Margaret Hayes. The Dean of Engineering is
Dr. Samuel Okafor.

The university has six faculties: Engineering, Sciences, Arts and Humanities,
Law, Medicine, and Business.

The main library, the Ashcombe Library, holds over 1.2 million printed volumes and
is open 24 hours a day during term time.

Tuition fees for undergraduate students are 9,250 pounds per year for UK students
and 22,000 pounds per year for international students.

The Computer Science department was ranked 5th nationally in the most recent
government research assessment.

The university's academic year is divided into three terms: Autumn, Spring, and
Summer. Autumn term begins in the last week of September.

Student accommodation on campus houses approximately 4,500 students across nine
residence halls.

The university's sports centre includes an Olympic-sized swimming pool and was
completed in 2011.
